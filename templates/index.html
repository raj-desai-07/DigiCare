About DigiCare

Heart Health Awareness Starts Here

DigiCare is a structured cardiovascular risk assessment platform designed to help individuals understand the vital health indicators most commonly evaluated during cardiac reviews. By guiding users through 11 clinically recognised parameters from resting ECG patterns and blood sugar levels to blood pressure and cholesterol observations DigiCare consolidates key health factors into an intuitive profile that prepares users for more informed, confident discussions with their clinicians.

Cardiovascular disease remains one of the world's most significant health concerns, yet many individuals attend clinical appointments without a clear understanding of their personal risk factors. DigiCare bridges this critical gap by translating complex medical metrics into accessible health awareness that complements professional medical care, encouraging proactive lifestyle habits and timely preventive consultations.
